# 06: Tick drawn topics

**What to build:** Players tick topics on their ticket that have been drawn. A tick on an undrawn topic is rejected with no change to the ticket.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Start the game and draw)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A player can tick a drawn topic on their own ticket
- [ ] A tick on an undrawn topic is rejected and leaves the ticket unchanged
- [ ] A tick on a topic not on the player's ticket is rejected
- [ ] A player can untick a topic before any claim is made
- [ ] Ticks are visible on the player's ticket
